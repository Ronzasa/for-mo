04 / The words I actually wanted to say

Dear Amogelang,

You know how I feel about the meaning of words. This is why I couldn't just say "happy birthday" and call it a day.

♡
A LETTER FOR MO · CHAPTER TWENTY-ONE

Damn, I really haven’t tapped into my writing side in a while. But because of the type of person you are, I believe your influence has brought me back to that state again. More than anything, I think it comes from understanding just how much words mean to you.

If I could describe how much your presence and friendship have changed me as a person, I don't even think you'd understand the extent of it. I genuinely wouldn't know where to start. But then again, that's not such a big deal, because I believe our friendship has long passed the stage of worrying about where things started.

You know, the phrase "you don't know what you mean to me" has become so cliché that, in some ways, it's lost its meaning. However, I, for one, don't have the luxury of throwing those words around. I value the meaning of words far too much. So when I tell you that you mean an astounding amount to me, understand that those aren't just words put together to sound beautiful. They come from the very depths of my soul.

Aggh, man. 😭

I live by the philosophy that "less is more." I keep a small circle because I value genuine connections over having a multitude of people around me. And you being part of that small circle means something. It means you're one of Ronie's favourites. Crazy, right? 😭

And trust me, it genuinely breaks my heart knowing that I might not be there for your 21st birthday celebration. It's an internal conflict I've been trying to resolve for weeks, and somehow, it still bugs the hell out of me.

But you know what? If I ever had the opportunity to introduce you to someone who had never met you, I imagine it would go something like this:

"Hey, stranger. This is Amogelang."

Don't ask me how we met, but trust me when I tell you that if you're looking for a friend who is both willing and able to listen, even when she doesn't have a solution to your problems, this is her.

If you're looking for a friend who would put herself last just to see you happy, this is her.

If you're looking for someone who will laugh at your jokes, understand your humour, and occasionally entertain your nonsense, this is her.

If you're looking for a friend who will take her time to understand the kind of human being you are, learn what you like and dislike, and make an effort to know you beyond the surface, this is her.

If you're looking for a friend who won't hesitate to tell you when you're invalidating her feelings, even when you're convinced you're right, this is Mo. 😭

If you're looking for someone filled with so much love, yet carrying such a soft and sensitive heart, this is her.

Damn, now I feel guilty about not being at your 21st, Mo. 😔

If you're looking for a friend who, even after being disappointed, will try to find it within herself to forgive you, this is her.

And trust me, stranger, I could do this all day. I could spend hours breaking down what a wonderful specimen of a human being she is. A person raised with love, good values, compassion, and so much more.

You see, one thing I've come to appreciate about our friendship is that we've had our fallouts, our misunderstandings, and moments when things weren't necessarily okay between us. Yet somehow, we always found our way back to each other.

And strangely enough, I would say those moments have been some of the most meaningful parts of our friendship. Not because I enjoyed them, but because they made me realise just how much this friendship means to me.

I won't sit here and act tough or nonchalant and pretend those days didn't bother me. Man, I was literally losing sleep over some of them. 😭

But that's not what this whole piece of writing is about.

This is about appreciating you, Mo.

I'm beyond lucky to have you as a friend, and I hope you understand that I will always be there for you, always rooting for you, and always wishing the absolute best for you, regardless of whatever circumstances this world may bring.

And you know me. I keep my promises.

Trust me, I've been grinding through my coursework, partly because I'm trying to see whether I can somehow squeeze in enough time to celebrate with you. It's funny because I find myself making efforts and doing things I wouldn't ordinarily do for anyone else. And I think that's how I know just how deeply I appreciate you.

I still go back and read the birthday message you sent me. And trust me, there are moments when tears genuinely settle at the edges of my eyes. But you know me, Mr Nonchalant. I wipe those away before anybody gets suspicious. 😭

It's crazy how words can carry so much weight when they come from someone who genuinely means them.

And I suppose that's what I've been trying to tell you throughout this whole message. It's not necessarily about how many words I can put together, or how beautifully I can arrange them. It's about the meaning behind them. It's about making sure that, even if I don't always express it, you know just how much I value your existence in my life.

I told you earlier that I live by "less is more."

Looking at the length of this message, I think I might have just violated one of my own principles. 😭

But then again, it's you.
Why wouldn't I?

With all that said, Amogelang, I wish you a great, wonderful, and lovely 21st birthday. Enjoy this year because, as much as birthdays come every year, turning 21 is a remarkable milestone, one that only comes once. I'm beyond glad that I get to be part of it.

Yeah, man, wishing you nothing but the best, and hopefully, you'll find it in your heart to forgive ngwanago for not being there. 😭❤️

Love,
Ronie Hokwana.
